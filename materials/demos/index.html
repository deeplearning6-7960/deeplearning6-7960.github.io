Thu 9/17 — Approximation theory

  • 01 — Approximation
    Fit a 1D target with a two-layer ReLU MLP. Slide H to see how more hidden units improve the piecewise-linear approximation.
  • 02 — Effect of ReLU on Kinks
    Shift different functions with bias and see how ReLU creates or removes kinks, including on the Telgarsky sawtooth.
  • 03 — Width vs Depth
    Fix the total neuron budget N, then vary the aspect ratio. Compare wide-shallow and narrow-deep networks on the same task.
  • 04 — Sinusoidal Positional Encoding
    Plain MLP versus a positional-encoding MLP on a noisy scatter plot. Drag H and L to see how the encoding unlocks high-frequency fitting.
